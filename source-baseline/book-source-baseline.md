check its contents against the edition manifest before building.

`book-source-baseline.env` is the machine-readable authority; `SOURCE-VERSION.md`
documents tagged checkouts and responding to content mismatches. The manifest records 1,448
file contents and retains observed commit provenance as evidence only. The patch
file is empty and its digest is verified. The checkout target is the immutable tag `Book-1.0`.

worker, together with the compiler's `-fno-gnu-unique` policy. Claim review and
build/runtime evidence are recorded in `review/pedagogical-smoothing-2026-09-23/`.
